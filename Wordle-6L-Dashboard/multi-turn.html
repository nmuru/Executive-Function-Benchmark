Evaluating executive function and information gain across multi-turn gameplay.

Task Insights

  • Data Source: evaluate_6Lwordle_multi_turn.
  • Win Rate vs. Info Gain: As games progress to the 6th turn, the task becomes easier and win rates naturally spike from 7-8% to 70-80%, reducing the relative importance of the Information Gain metric compared to single-turn evaluations.
  • Frontier Dominance in State Tracking: The top five models remain largely consistent but reorder: GPT-5.5 takes second place and Claude Opus overtakes Gemma-4-31b. This shift occurs because multi-turn gameplay demands superior working memory and dynamic state tracking, giving larger frontier models an edge.
  • Inhibitory Control: Rule violations scale proportionally with overall scores, demonstrating that high-performing models possess superior working memory management and inhibitory control.
  • Mid-Weight Efficiency: Gemma-4 maintains strong performance across both modes, ranking second in cost, speed, and info density despite being the slowest model tested.
  • Non-Reasoning Shortfalls: Grok non-reasoning falls far behind the pack, failing on both performance scores and cost efficiency.
