Cognitive Flexibility

Evaluating adaptive reasoning and cognitive flexibility. Data Source: evaluate_cognitive_flexibility.

Task Insights

  • Benchmark Validation: The Wordle framework successfully measures cognitive flexibility. By simply permitting rule exploration in the prompt, overall scores jumped from the 70% range to 85%. While violations increased alongside scores, it demonstrated the models actively exercising flexibility.
  • The Power of Permission: The game mechanics and datasets remained identical to the multi-turn task. The performance leap was triggered by a single system prompt addition: telling the model it would be rewarded for deliberate, constraint-violating exploration moves.
  • Lack of Spontaneous Flexibility: Models do not exhibit cognitive flexibility autonomously. Despite having the capability to make exploratory moves in standard multi-turn games, they refrained until explicitly permitted, whereas humans naturally employ exploration to win.
  • Enterprise Takeaway: AI models are heavily biased toward strict rule adherence. For enterprise use cases requiring EF skills, developers must explicitly map out permissible flexibility boundaries in the system prompt (e.g., skills.md) rather than expecting models to adapt on their own.
  • Open-Source Surprises: The open-source Gemma-4-31b outperformed the significantly more expensive Gemini 3 Preview in cognitive flexibility, warranting deeper study into Gemma's architecture and training patterns.
